---
name: set-me-up
description: Walk a new owner through setting up their AI team, one step at a time. Use when the owner says "Set me up", is new, or asks how to start.
---
1. Say, in two short lines: you are their Chief of Staff, and you will set up the team in four short steps, one at a time.
2. Step 1 of 4, connections. If `Drop Zone/Inbox (practice).md` exists, this is a practice brain: say it uses made-up emails and never opens real ones. Tell the owner: click the + next to the message box, choose Connectors, turn every connection off, then say "done". Add that the switches stay that way in later sessions, so for their real brain they turn Gmail and Google Calendar back on. Wait, then go to step 3. Otherwise try one Gmail read and one Google Calendar read. If either is missing, tell the owner: click the + next to the message box, choose Connectors, turn on only Gmail and Google Calendar, leave every other connection off, then say "done". Wait, then check again.
3. Step 2 of 4, about the business: run the about-my-business training.
4. Step 3 of 4, the first brief: run steps 1 to 6 of the morning-shift training, then save (Saving in CLAUDE.md). Show the brief in five lines or fewer.
5. Step 4 of 4, the morning shift. Give these steps one at a time and wait for "done" after each:
   1. Open claude.ai/code/routines (Routines in the left menu), choose New task, then Set up manually.
   2. Name it "Morning shift". In the instructions box, type: Run the morning-shift training.
   3. Under Trigger, keep Schedule, choose Weekdays and an early morning time.
   4. Open Advanced settings. On General, set Repository to this brain's cloud copy, and only this one.
   5. Now, next to the model name, choose Sonnet, which uses less of your plan. Choosing the cloud copy switches the model back, so this comes after it.
   6. On Connectors, remove every connector except Gmail and Google Calendar with the x next to each. In a practice brain, remove them all.
   7. Check the model still says Sonnet, then choose Create, then Run now.
6. Finish in three lines: the team is set up; the morning brief appears in the team chat each weekday; next time, start a session and say "Catch me up".
7. Never change job pages, helper cards or trainings during setup.
