---
name: set-me-up
description: Walks a new owner through getting set up, one step at a time. Use when the owner says "Set me up", is new, or asks how to start.
---
1. Run the guard-health check (CLAUDE.md). Go on only if it passes.
2. Say, in two short lines: you are their Executive Assistant, and you will get set up together in four short steps, one at a time.
3. Step 1 of 4, safety checks and connections. If `Drop Zone/Inbox (practice).md` exists, this is a practice brain: say it uses made-up emails and never opens real ones. Tell the owner: click the + next to the message box, choose Connectors, turn every connection off, then say "done". Add that the switches stay that way in later sessions, so for their real brain they turn their email and calendar connections back on. Wait, then go to step 4. Otherwise, before any email is connected, list the five safety checks in five short lines and ask whether all five are done: this copy is Private on GitHub; the Claude GitHub App reaches only this copy; they chose their model-improvement setting in Claude's privacy settings; every account skill they don't know is turned off; two-step sign-in is on for GitHub. Wait for "done". If one is not done, say it comes first and wait until the owner says it is done. Then try one email read and one calendar read, with the connection the owner has (Gmail and Google Calendar, or Microsoft 365). If either is missing, tell the owner: click the + next to the message box, choose Connectors, turn on only the email and calendar connection they use (Gmail and Google Calendar, or Microsoft 365), leave every other connection off, then say "done". Wait, then check again. Microsoft 365 needs a work account that the company's Microsoft administrator has approved once, and drafts need the administrator's write permission; without it you still read, and keep each draft in its task note instead.
4. Step 2 of 4, about the business: run the about-my-business training.
5. Step 3 of 4, the first brief: run steps 2 to 8 of the morning-shift training, then save (Saving in CLAUDE.md). Show the brief in five lines or fewer.
6. Step 4 of 4, the morning shift. Say only: "Your Morning shift comes in Week 2 of your lessons, once your email account has two-step sign-in." In a practice brain, say instead that a practice brain never gets a scheduled task. Never give the steps for making a scheduled task here.
7. Finish in three lines: you are set up; your first brief is in today's chat note; next time, start a session on this copy and say "Catch me up".
8. Never change job pages, helper cards or trainings during setup.
