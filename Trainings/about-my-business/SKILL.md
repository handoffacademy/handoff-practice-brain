---
name: about-my-business
description: Interviews the owner about the business and fills in the Business notes. Use when the owner says "Start the About My Business interview" or wants to tell you about the business.
---
1. Tell the owner: eight short questions, one at a time, and they can answer in their own words or say "skip".
2. Ask one question, wait for the answer, write it into the right note, then ask the next:
   1. What do you sell, and to whom? (About the Business, Customers)
   2. What does each offer cost? (Offers: one line per offer with its price)
   3. How do customers find you? (About the Business)
   4. Which days and hours do you work, and which are your days off? Busy and quiet seasons? (About the Business)
   5. How should your assistant sound? How do you sign off? (Voice)
   6. Which tools do you use for email, calendar, payments? (About the Business)
   7. Any rules for your kind of business? (About the Business)
   8. What must your assistant never do or promise? (About the Business)
3. Keep the owner's words. Never add a fact the owner did not give. Leave a skipped question as `(not answered yet)`.
4. Update `Business/index.md` and add `## [<today>] interview | About My Business` to `Business/log.md`.
5. Finish with a five-line summary and ask what is wrong or missing.
