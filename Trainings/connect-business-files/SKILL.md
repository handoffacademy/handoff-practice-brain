---
name: connect-business-files
description: Help the owner choose whether their everyday assistant may read business files.
---
1. Only when the owner asks in this session. Never during a scheduled shift. Read `.handoff/brain.json`. If `practice` is true, explain that this copy uses made-up documents and never opens real accounts. Run the use-business-files training with the practice files instead. Do not change the switches.
2. Explain: "I can find information in your business files and use it to prepare answers, meeting notes and customer replies. You choose whether to connect Google Drive or SharePoint and OneDrive. I won't edit or share those files, and nothing sends without you." Explain that the connection can read files their connected account is allowed to read, so they should connect only an appropriate business account. Ask which connection they want, or whether they want to leave files off. Wait.
3. Explain the requirements for the chosen provider: Google Drive is separate from Gmail and Google Calendar. SharePoint and OneDrive use Microsoft 365 alongside Outlook; that needs a work or school account and the company's administrator approval. Do not promise that a paid plan or a connection guarantees access to every file. If permission is missing, ask the owner to arrange access with their administrator or paste an approved excerpt. Never ask for passwords, share a file publicly, reconnect with broader permissions, or work around an access error.
4. Ask the owner to choose Accept edits in the menu next to the model name. Read `.handoff/brain.json` again. Preserve every existing field exactly, including name, backupRemote, practice and connections. Add or change only `fileAccess`, with exactly two boolean fields: `googleDrive` and `microsoftFiles`. Keep each unchosen switch at its current true/false value, or false if absent. Turning a connection off uses the same process. Show the owner the proposed on/off choices in plain words, then use Write to write the whole file. The guard presents an approval card: ask the owner to check the choices and choose Allow once only if they want those file reads. If denied, stop; do not retry or claim access is enabled.
5. Read the marker back and report the saved choices. Save using CLAUDE.md. Ask the owner to open +, then Connectors, and enable only their chosen file connection alongside their existing email and calendar connections. Leave all unrelated connections off. Switching a provider on in Claude is separate from the brain's file-read choice; both are needed. Do not alter account-wide permissions on the owner's behalf.
6. Ask for the name or link of one safe business document. Use only the approved read tools in use-business-files to look it up. Confirm the document's title and link before reading it if there are several matches. An unavailable tool, denied permission, missing file or unreadable content is a failed check, not an empty document. Explain what happened and offer an approved excerpt or the offline exercise.
7. When that read works, offer the three exercises: find an answer, prepare for a meeting, draft a customer reply. Do not add file searches to the morning or Friday shift, change schedules, or hire a specialist. The Chief can do all three tasks itself.
