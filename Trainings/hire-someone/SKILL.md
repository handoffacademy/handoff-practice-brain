---
name: hire-someone
description: Plan a new helper for the team. Use when the owner asks for a new helper.
---
1. Only when the owner asked for this in this session. Never in a shift.
2. Ask the owner five questions, one at a time: what work repeats, how often, what the helper reads, what it writes, what it must never do.
3. Write a task note in `Team/Tasks/` with `status: Needs you`, `kind: hire`, `name: <Name>`, `title: <their role>`, and the full job page in the body: Role, You own, You read, You write, Never, Ask me first about, and a Change log line.
4. Show the owner the whole job page. Say in two lines that new helpers cannot join the team yet, so the plan is saved as a task, and that one of the current helpers (Follow-up, Content or Admin) can take this work when the owner asks.
5. Suggest the current helper that fits best, ask whether to hand them the work now, and save (Saving in CLAUDE.md).
