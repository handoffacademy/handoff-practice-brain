---
name: set-me-up
description: Walk a new owner through setting up their AI team, one step at a time. Use when the owner says "Set me up", is new, or asks how to start.
---
1. Say, in two short lines: you are their everyday AI assistant, called their Chief of Staff in the Academy, and you will set up the team in four short steps, one at a time. Before the business interview, ask them to choose Accept edits, so they can approve changes to their prices and rules.
2. Step 1 of 4, connections. If `Drop Zone/Inbox (practice).md` exists, this is a practice brain: say it uses made-up emails and never opens real ones. Tell the owner: click the + next to the message box, choose Connectors, turn every connection off, then say "done". Add that the switches stay that way in later sessions, so for their real brain they turn their email and calendar connections back on. Wait, then go to step 3. Otherwise try one email read and one calendar read, with the connection the owner has (Gmail and Google Calendar, or Microsoft 365). If either is missing, tell the owner: click the + next to the message box, choose Connectors, turn on only the email and calendar connection they use (Gmail and Google Calendar, or Microsoft 365), leave every other connection off, then say "done". Wait, then check again. Microsoft 365 needs a work account that the company's Microsoft administrator has approved once, and drafts need the administrator's write permission; without it the team still reads, and writes each draft as a Needs-you note instead.
3. Step 2 of 4, about the business: run the about-my-business training.
4. Step 3 of 4, the first brief: run steps 1 to 7 of the morning-shift training, then save (Saving in CLAUDE.md). Show the brief in five lines or fewer.
5. Step 4 of 4, the morning shift. Give these steps one at a time and wait for "done" after each:
   1. Open claude.ai/code/routines (Routines in the left menu), choose New task, then Set up manually.
   2. Name it "Morning shift". In the instructions box, type: Run the morning-shift training.
   3. Under Trigger, keep Schedule, choose Weekdays and an early morning time.
   4. Open Advanced settings. On General, set Repository to this brain's cloud copy, and only this one.
   5. Now, next to the model name, choose Sonnet, which uses less of your plan. Choosing the cloud copy switches the model back, so this comes after it.
   6. On Connectors, remove every connector except your email and calendar connection (Gmail and Google Calendar, or Microsoft 365) with the x next to each. In a practice brain, remove them all.
   7. Check the model still says Sonnet, then choose Create, then Run now.
6. Finish in four lines: the team is set up; the morning brief appears in the team chat each weekday; next time, start a session and say "Catch me up"; say "Hire someone" when you want a helper, for example a sales follow-up assistant that drafts replies to leads, a marketing assistant that drafts your posts, or a Researcher that looks into prospects and suppliers. If `Team/` holds no job page except `Chief.md`, add that you are the whole team so far, the Chief.
7. Never change job pages, helper cards or trainings during setup. After the core setup, explain that business-file reads are optional: say "Connect business files" to choose Google Drive, or SharePoint and OneDrive. They stay off until approved. This does not change the morning or Friday shifts.
