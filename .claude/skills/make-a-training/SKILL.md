---
name: make-a-training
description: Turn a routine into a written procedure the team follows the same way every time. Use when the owner asks to teach the team a procedure.
---
1. Only when the owner asked for this in this session. Never in a shift.
2. Ask the owner which routine, or read the `How We Work/` note they name.
3. Write it as short numbered steps: what to read, what to write, what never to do. Drafts only; nothing is sent.
4. Show the owner the whole procedure and ask "Save this procedure?" Wait for the answer.
5. If the owner says yes, write it to `How We Work/<name of the procedure>.md`, with the first line `Written with the owner on <today>.`, then save (Saving in CLAUDE.md).
6. Tell the owner in two lines: the procedure is saved, and whenever they want the team to use it, they say "Follow the <name> procedure" and you will show it to them first.
