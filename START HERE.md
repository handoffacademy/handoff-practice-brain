# Start here

This is your brain: everything your AI team knows about the business.
To talk to your team, open claude.ai/code, start a session on this copy, and type "Catch me up".
New here? Type "Set me up" and your Chief walks you through it, one step at a time.
Drop anything new into **Drop Zone**. Your Chief organizes it; changes to prices and rules need your approval.
Want help from business files? Say "Connect business files" to choose Google Drive, or SharePoint and OneDrive. File reads start off. Then ask your assistant to find an answer, prepare for a meeting or draft a reply with its sources. Nothing sends without you.
Anything that needs you is a task marked "Needs you" in **Team/Tasks**.
