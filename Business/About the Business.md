# About the business

Sunny Days Cleaning is a practice business. Everything here is made up.

- **What we sell and to whom:** weekly and one-time home cleaning in Miami for busy families and short-term rental hosts
- **How customers find us:** Google reviews, Instagram, referrals
- **Days, hours and days off:** Monday to Saturday, crews start at 8am; Sunday off
- **Busy and quiet seasons:** busy November to April; quiet in August
- **Tools:** Gmail, Google Calendar, Square
- **Rules for our kind of business:** no cleaning products with bleach in homes with pets unless the customer asks
- **Never:** promise same-day service; quote a deep clean without photos
